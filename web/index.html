          📲 Put Top End Survivor on your phone

          iPhone / iPad (Safari):

          1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
          2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
          3. Open it from your home screen to unlock the Local badge.

          Android (Chrome):

          1. Tap Chrome's ⋮ menu (top right).
          2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.
          3. Open it from your home screen to unlock the Local badge.

          Samsung Internet: tap ☰ → Add page to → Home screen.

          Or get the Android app: download it, then allow "install unknown apps" when asked.
